The Shopify app

Five screens from stranger to customer. The scan is the hook, the free project is the proof — and "requote everything" is the upgrade. Exactly what a seller sees, in order.

01

Their store — where it starts

Nothing new to learn. acmegoods runs on Shopify like any other store — 34 products, and the top seller costs $11.20/unit landed. The sourcing problem is invisible: it just lives quietly in the margin. No apps installed yet.

02

One install, from the Shopify App Store

Free plan, no card. The listing promises exactly three things: an instant savings scan, one free test project, and an employee — not a directory — who does the work. Thirty seconds from search to installed.

03

The scan — instant estimates on install

Minutes after install, the whole catalog has been read against 4M+ vetted factories: ~$18,400/yr in likely savings, per-product ranges with confidence. And one honesty row — the camp mug is "already at market — nice work." That row is what makes the other numbers believable.

04

The free test project — the engine, visible

One product, real RFQs. Kai emails three vetted factories from the store's own domain, chases the quiet one politely in its timezone, and files every reply to the project. Updates land where the seller already works — WhatsApp, Slack, or an email digest. Nothing sends without approval.

05

Quotes in — the estimate, proven

The trust moment: we estimated $8.50–9.20 — best real quote $8.90 ✓. Three normalized quotes, Kai's pick highlighted, ~$11,500/yr on this one SKU. From here it's samples for $86, credited to the first PO — and 11 more products with findings waiting behind the Growth plan.
