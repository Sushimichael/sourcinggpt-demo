01

Install the connector

In Claude (or any MCP client): add the sourcinggpt connector. Your AI can discover it on its own — sourcinggpt.ai/llms.txt lists every capability.

02

Give it a desk

Create a dedicated project — “Sourcing” — and paste your company profile: products, target margins, incoterms, preferred ports, existing suppliers. That project is your sourcing department now.

03

Talk to it like your supply-chain manager

It comes with its own software built in — RFQs, quotations to your own customers, sample orders, QC inspection, shipment booking. You approve; it executes.
